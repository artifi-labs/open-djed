# Milestone 7 - Maintenance & Support for Months 9-11

- **Submitted:** OnGoing
- **Milestone Cost:** 10% (ADA 10,000)
- **Milestone Link:** [Milestone 7](https://milestones.projectcatalyst.io/projects/1400095/milestones/7)

---

## Milestone Outputs

The milestone outputs for Project Close-out are:

- Final close-out report
- Final close-out video
- Maintenance of Open DJED and user support for the 12th month after proposal start.

## Acceptance Criteria

To consider this milestone completed:

- Final close-out report is publicly available
- Final close-out report includes two extra sections: 1. Covering mitigation of USD-denominated budget shortfalls due to unexpected ADA price volatility 2. Covering successful navigation of unforeseen technical challenges, DJED protocol changes or Cardano hardforks and the activation of the Contingency & Volatility Buffer; Additionally the report will include a short description & justification of whether or not the contingency buffer was significantly used or not and why
- Final close-out video is publicly available
- The close-out report and video comply with the formats and standards set by Project Catalyst
- For the period spanning the 12th month after proposal start:
  - Public up-time dashboard shows >= 99% availability
  - Minimal service interruptions take place
  - GitHub issues tagged as "bug" are triaged within 48 hours and resolved in subsequent releases
  - The user support channels on the Artifi community Discord are actively monitored and response times are under 24 hours for critical inquiries

## Evidence of Completion

Completion is evidenced by:

- Link to final close-out report
- Link to final close-out video

- Link to the GitHub repository (https://github.com/artifi-labs/open-djed) showing bugfix pull requests and resolved issues
- Screenshots or links to public conversations on public channels (Discord/GitHub)
